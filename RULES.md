# 🕵️ BLACKBOX — THE LOST FILE
### A Cyber Mystery Game by ASTRA Association

---

## What is BLACKBOX?

BLACKBOX is a **web-based digital investigation game** where your team follows a trail of hidden clues left behind by a mysterious entity known only as **NULL**. A critical file has disappeared from the Black Box Association Archive — your mission is to find it.

Each stage teaches you a real-world cybersecurity concept through hands-on discovery. You don't need to know anything about cybersecurity to play — just curiosity, teamwork, and a browser.

> *"You didn't hack the system. You simply learned how to look at it."*

## 🏁 How to Play

### Registration
1. Open the game website on your team's laptop
2. Enter a **unique team name** (max 50 characters)
3. Click **[ BEGIN INVESTIGATION ]** to start

### Gameplay
- Each stage presents a **mystery** and a hidden clue
- Solve the puzzle to discover a **keyword** — enter it to progress
- Stages are **sequential** — you must solve each one before moving to the next
- A **hint system** is available if you get stuck (3 progressive hints per stage)
- Your progress is saved automatically — you can refresh without losing anything

### Winning
- The first team to **complete all 10 stages** wins
- Final rankings are based on **completion time**
- A live **leaderboard** shows all teams' progress in real time

## 📜 Rules

### ✅ Allowed
1. **Use your browser's built-in tools** — Inspect Element, View Source, Console, DevTools are all fair game
2. **Search the internet** for help with techniques (e.g., "how to decode Base64")
3. **Discuss and collaborate** within your own team
4. **Take notes** — some clues from earlier stages are needed later
5. **Use the hint system** — hints won't disqualify you (but speed matters!)

### ❌ Not Allowed
1. **No sharing answers** between teams — this is a competition
2. **No accessing the admin dashboard** (`/admin`) — that's for organizers only
3. **No tampering with other teams** — don't mess with others' sessions or progress
4. **No using AI/ChatGPT to directly solve puzzles** — the point is to learn the concepts yourself
5. **No brute-forcing answers** — the system rate-limits submissions (5 attempts per minute)
6. **No accessing the game's source code repository** — that contains all answers

### ⚠️ Important Notes
- Answers are **case-sensitive** and always **lowercase**
- Each answer is a **single word or hyphenated phrase** (no spaces)
- If you're stuck, **use the hints** — they're designed to guide you without giving away the answer
- **Refreshing the page** won't reset your progress
- If you encounter a technical issue, raise your hand and an organizer will help

## 🎯 Tips for Success

1. **Read everything carefully** — clues hide in plain sight
2. **Right-click is your best friend** — "View Page Source" and "Inspect" open new worlds
3. **Don't skip the story text** — it often contains direct hints about the mechanic
4. **Take notes on keywords** — you'll need earlier discoveries in the final stage
5. **Use hints wisely** — the first hint is vague, the third nearly gives it away
6. **Split tasks** — one person reads the story, another inspects the code
7. **Stay calm** — if something seems impossible, you're probably overthinking it

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What if my team gets completely stuck?**
A: Use the hint system! Every stage has 3 progressive hints. If you're still stuck, an organizer can provide guidance.

**Q: Can we use our phones?**
A: You can use phones to search for help online, but the game itself should be played on a laptop (some challenges require DevTools which aren't easily accessible on mobile).

**Q: What happens if we accidentally close the tab?**
A: Don't worry! Your progress is saved. Just reopen the game URL and you'll continue from where you left off.

**Q: Is this actual hacking?**
A: No. Everything in this game is intentionally designed to be discovered. You're not breaking into anything — you're learning to look at technology the way security professionals do.

**Q: Can we have more than 3 team members?**
A: The recommended max is 3 to ensure everyone gets hands-on experience. Larger teams tend to have members who just watch.
